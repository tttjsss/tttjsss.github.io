Frame Sync: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

What the app accesses

Frame Sync uses the Google Photos Library API with these permissions only:

  • add photos to the signed-in user's Google Photos library, into an album the app creates;
  • view and manage photos and albums that the app itself created.

It cannot see or change any other photos or albums in the account. It also reads the signed-in account's email address, only to confirm the owner signed in with the intended account.

How the data is used and stored

Photos are copied only into the app's own album so that a household photo frame can display them. The app runs on the owner's own computer. Its sign-in token and the list of copied photos are stored there, and nowhere else.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics. Nothing is sent anywhere except Google Photos.

Frame Sync's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The account owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The app's album stays in Google Photos until the owner deletes it.
